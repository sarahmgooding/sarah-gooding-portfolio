Jarrah

Social Media Management, Content Strategy, and Content Creation

  • Social
  • Video
  • Content Strategy
  • B2C

I grew Jarrah’s Instagram following 34% in four months (July to Oct 2026), while sustaining an established 5.5K Facebook community. The Instagram page achieved an 8.9% engagement rate, and nearly 1 in 5 profile visitors went on to follow the account.

I developed and executed social media content strategy, managed content calendars, and created UGC-style content supporting multiple product roll-outs. This involved shooting and editing video, writing scripts and captions, sourcing music, and designing posts in Canva. I then scheduled posts in Meta Business Suite and managed the community across Facebook and Instagram.

I also managed strategic UGC creator and influencer partnerships. This entailed packaging and sending product gift boxes and collaborating with select content creators on posts to seed the brand into the community.
