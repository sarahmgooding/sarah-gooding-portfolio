I was the founding Creative Copywriter on The FRIENDS Experience, an immersive celebration of the iconic 90s TV show, created by Superfly, Warner Bros, and HBO Max.

Drawing on my deep knowledge of pop culture, I developed the brand voice for the experience, which was executed throughout all customer-facing content, including experiential, press, marketing, and advertising. I created all signage for the experience, from welcome messaging to wall-mounted exhibit placards, interactive games, and retail signage.

I also generated 360 marketing concepts, with a focus on social, digital, and OOH. This involved populating editorial calendars for Instagram, Facebook and Twitter, and writing email marketing copy throughout the marketing funnel.

I also wrote all copy for the official website, including the home page, FAQ, ticketing, and e-commerce store. I wrote the product descriptions in a way that referenced show moments that would resonate with fans. My writing also spanned Google search copy, promotional scripts for TV and radio, and sponsored editorial content for partner websites.

Since the flagship location opened in New York City in 2021, The FRIENDS Experience has expanded internationally to locations including the UK, Australia, Canada, France, Brazil, and the Philippines.
