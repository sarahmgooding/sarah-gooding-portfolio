Purpose-Driven Travel

Purpose-Driven Travel Content Strategy and Copywriting

  • Digital
  • Video
  • Content Strategy

I worked with American Express Travel to build a Purpose-Driven Travel microsite that features their partner hotels, resorts, and tour providers that focus on sustainability.

To begin, I combed through their vast catalogue of partners to vet and select those that met American Express's strict criteria. Then I created key categories that I organized the partners into, enabling our audience to identify and search for properties and tours based on their priorities (such as Community Impact, Environmental Focus, Marine Conservation, Animal Conservation, and Cultural Heritage). Then I wrote copy for each partner hotel and tour, highlighting three key features or experiences they offered. These linked to additional pages with broader information that I also helped curate and create.

The resulting microsite is highly interactive, educational, and simple to use, with direct links to the American Express booking portal, allowing the audience to reserve accommodation and experiences quickly and easily.
