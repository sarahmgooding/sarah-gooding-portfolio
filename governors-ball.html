Governors Ball

Live stream scriptwriting

I worked on the festival’s 2022 live stream as a scriptwriter, both remotely in the lead-up to the festival, and on-site during the festival.

I wrote all content for the live stream, including daily scripts for the hosts; interview questions for artists, talent, and special guests on set and in the field; on-screen supers; and promotional content. I also ideated and wrote trivia, games, competitions, and other interactive content to engage vendors, partners, and viewers.

Throughout the festival I also wrote and published live stream chat comments in the voice of the festival.
