Begin Again campaign

With travel restrictions lifted following the pandemic, American Express Travel wanted a campaign that would speak to the moment and help it tactfully lead the way in the return to travel.

Through focus groups and consumer research, we settled on the campaign platform, Begin Again, which empowered safe, meaningful travel for those who were ready to travel, and gave inspiration to those who were not quite ready yet.

Using a customizable fill-in-the-blank device, we allowed Amex card members to project their own memories into a rotating selection of actions they were encouraged to begin again, from planning and booking trips to adventuring and making memories.

Begin Again was brought to life through B2B and B2C collateral including social, print, and video. The optimistic tone set a new direction for the brand and continues to inform its consumer-facing messaging to this day.

The campaign helped American Express Travel outperform its competitors in spend recovery as well as its own 2019 booking levels in 2021-2022.

Purpose-Driven Travel Microsite

I worked with American Express Travel to build a Purpose-Driven Travel microsite that features their partner hotels, resorts, and tour providers that focus on sustainability.

To begin, I combed through their vast catalogue of partners to vet and select those that met American Express's strict criteria. Then I created key categories that I organized the partners into, enabling our audience to identify and search for properties and tours based on their priorities (such as Community Impact, Environmental Focus, Marine Conservation, Animal Conservation, and Cultural Heritage). Then I wrote copy for each partner hotel and tour, highlighting three key features or experiences they offered. These linked to additional pages with broader information that I also helped curate and create.

The resulting microsite is highly interactive, educational, and simple to use, with direct links to the American Express booking portal, allowing the audience to reserve accommodation and experiences quickly and easily.
